About

I'm a PhD student in Mathematics (specialty E7). I came to mathematics from computer science: I did my bachelor's at Igor Sikorsky Kyiv Polytechnic Institute and my master's at the Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics of Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv.

My research is in dynamical systems: impulsive dynamical systems, control theory and global attractors. I also write a lot of code, mostly in Python with the scientific stack.

What I do

Most of my time goes to research, but I also take on work like this. If you need something done, message me on Telegram.

Python & data analysis
